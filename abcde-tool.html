About the ABCDE Tool

The ABCDE mnemonic provides a rapid, bedside-friendly screening approach to identify patients at risk for ERAS failure. Each letter represents a key domain of risk factors. This simplified tool is designed for quick clinical triage when detailed calculator input is not immediately feasible.
