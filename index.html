About This Calculator

This tool predicts the risk of standard ERAS (Enhanced Recovery After Surgery) failure in patients undergoing emergency surgery for acute small bowel obstruction. The prediction model was developed and validated using a multicenter cohort study and is intended for preoperative risk stratification and clinical decision support.

Model Information

Development Cohort: N = 640 (Training), 7:3 internal validation, external validation cohort

Model Type: Multivariate Logistic Regression with LASSO variable selection (λ.1se, 10-fold CV)

Performance: AUC = 0.924 (Training), 0.935 (Internal Validation), 0.907 (External Validation)

Calibration: Hosmer-Lemeshow test p > 0.05; well-calibrated across all cohorts

Clinical Utility: Net benefit demonstrated by Decision Curve Analysis
